## 📖 About the Project

**Med Imaging Hub** is a student-created educational platform built to make medical imaging concepts easier to understand for students, beginners, and anyone interested in the field.

Medical imaging is a broad field, and as a student, I experienced how difficult it can be to find explanations that are both simple and scientifically accurate.

That experience inspired me to create a space where students can explore medical imaging concepts, discover useful learning resources, and learn about the connection between medical imaging and artificial intelligence.

This website is built from my learning, curiosity, and the desire to make the journey a little easier for the students who come after me.

> *Learning medical imaging. Exploring new ideas. Sharing knowledge.*

## 🎯 Our Purpose

Med Imaging Hub is more than a personal portfolio. It is an initiative created with the aim of supporting the medical imaging learning community.

Through this platform, I share what I learn from my teachers, self-study, and practical exploration, while continuously improving the website with new content and resources.

The goal is simple: **make learning more accessible, understandable, and useful.**

## 🤖 Featured AI Projects

My interest in medical imaging extends beyond learning concepts. I also explore how artificial intelligence can be applied to practical medical imaging challenges.

Some of my projects include:

### 1. AI-Based X-ray Quality Control Classifier

An AI project designed to classify different X-ray image quality issues, including blur, exposure errors, and foreign artifacts.

### 2. AI-Assisted Breast Ultrasound Analysis

An exploratory deep learning project focused on breast ultrasound image classification and model-based visual explanations.

These projects are part of my ongoing learning and experimentation in AI and medical imaging. They are not intended to replace professional clinical assessment.
