About Med Imaging Hub

A student-led learning space exploring medical imaging, artificial intelligence, and healthcare technology.

Why Med Imaging Hub?

Medical imaging is constantly evolving. New imaging technologies, artificial intelligence, and digital healthcare tools are changing the way we understand and work with medical images.

Med Imaging Hub was created to bring these ideas together in one simple and accessible space — especially for students and beginners who want to learn, explore, and understand how technology connects with medical imaging.

What You'll Find Here

The Hub brings together educational articles, practical AI projects, useful learning resources, and insights into emerging technologies in medical imaging and healthcare.
