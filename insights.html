A model learns from examples. It can become very good at recognizing patterns associated with a particular outcome — but that does not necessarily mean it understands the image in the same way a human does.
A thought

This is one of the things I find most interesting about medical imaging AI. A correct prediction does not always tell us what the model has learned.

INSIGHT 03

Can AI be confident and still be wrong?

Sometimes the number beside a prediction can be misleading.

+
AI prediction 99%
High confidence ≠ guaranteed correctness
A model can be very confident in a prediction and still make a mistake.
In medical imaging, confidence should not be treated as proof. AI outputs need appropriate validation, clinical context, and human oversight.
A thought

A high percentage can look reassuring. But if AI is going to assist healthcare professionals, I think one of the important questions is not only “How accurate is it?” but also “When is it wrong?”
