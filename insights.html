Questions Worth Exploring

Some of the most interesting discoveries begin with a simple question.

Can AI make medical imaging workflows more efficient?
The interesting part is not simply whether AI can analyse an image, but how it could fit safely into the real clinical workflow.
+
How can AI-assisted imaging remain explainable?
A useful system should provide more than a prediction. Understanding why a model reaches a result is an important part of responsible clinical AI.
+
What could medical imaging look like in the next decade?
Faster acquisition, improved reconstruction, portable systems, quantitative imaging and AI-assisted workflows may all contribute to the next generation of imaging.
+
Where does a medical imaging professional fit into this future?
As imaging becomes more technology-driven, clinical understanding, patient care, image quality and technological awareness remain important parts of the profession.
+
