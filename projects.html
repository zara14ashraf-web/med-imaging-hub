PROJECT 01

X-ray Quality Control Classifier

Vision Transformer (ViT) • X-ray Image Quality Assessment

What was the idea?

This project explored whether artificial intelligence could help identify the quality of X-ray images before they are used for further interpretation or analysis.

I developed a Vision Transformer-based classifier to distinguish between acceptable and poor-quality X-ray images. The project helped me explore how deep learning can be applied to an important part of the medical imaging workflow: image quality control.

Model Vision Transformer
Dataset 1,866 X-ray Images
Focus Image Quality Control

Results

96.08% Validation Accuracy
92.31% Test Accuracy

This was one of my first projects exploring the connection between medical imaging and artificial intelligence, and it gave me a practical understanding of how a deep learning model can be trained to assess medical image quality.

PROJECT 02

Breast Ultrasound AI Analysis

BUS-BRA • EfficientNet-B3 • Grad-CAM++

What was the idea?

This project explored whether deep learning could assist in classifying breast ultrasound images as benign or malignant, while also providing a visual indication of where the model was focusing.

I developed an EfficientNet-B3 based classifier using the BUS-BRA breast ultrasound dataset. Alongside classification, I used Grad-CAM++ to explore the model's attention and compare it with the actual lesion region.

Dataset BUS-BRA
Model EfficientNet-B3
Explainability Grad-CAM++

Image-Level Results

88.35% Accuracy
81.90% Precision
83.50% Recall
82.69% F1-Score
0.9364 ROC-AUC

Case-Level Results

90.00% Accuracy
81.67% Precision
89.09% Recall
85.22% F1-Score
0.9505 ROC-AUC

Why explainability?

A prediction alone does not tell us what the model was looking at. Grad-CAM++ was therefore used to visualize the areas that contributed to the model's prediction and compare its attention with the annotated lesion regions.
