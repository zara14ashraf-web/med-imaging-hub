Joint master's programmes delivered by international university partnerships. Students from around the world can apply, with full scholarships available for selected students. Programme-specific eligibility applies.

Commonwealth Master's Scholarships

United Kingdom · Master's

Supports eligible students from Commonwealth countries to pursue full-time taught master's study in the UK. Check the current eligible countries, subjects, and university requirements before applying.

Chevening Scholarships

United Kingdom · One-Year Master's

A UK government scholarship for eligible applicants pursuing a one-year master's degree. Applicants need at least 2,800 hours of work experience, roughly equivalent to two years, gained after completing their undergraduate degree. The requirement must be met before applying.

2 Years Work Experience Required View Scholarship →

Chinese Government Scholarship

China · Master's & Doctoral Study

Scholarship opportunities for international students pursuing degree programmes at participating Chinese universities. For the Pakistan HEC route, master's applicants must have a 16-year bachelor's degree and be under 35 years of age. University and programme requirements also apply.

HEC / CSC Route View Scholarship →

DAAD

Germany · Master's & Study Opportunities

The German Academic Exchange Service provides scholarship opportunities and a programme database for international students. Relevant programmes in Medical Technology and related fields can be explored through the official programme database.

Programme-Dependent Explore DAAD →

Fulbright Scholarship

United States · Master's & PhD

A competitive scholarship programme for Pakistani students pursuing graduate study in the United States. Applicants should review the current USEFP eligibility criteria, degree requirements, test requirements, deadlines, and field restrictions before applying.

Fully Funded View Scholarship →
